PT bot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

PT bot is a personal application used only by its owner, Luke Hamilton, to track his own training and health. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, PT bot reads (read-only) the following from the Google Health API: heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, activity and fitness, body measurements (weight, body fat) and nutrition logs.

How data is used

The data is used solely to give the owner training, recovery and nutrition feedback. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and security

Access tokens and retrieved data are stored privately on a secured machine controlled by the owner. Data is kept only as long as needed for this purpose.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

PT bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
